# Movements

<Canvas of={Stories.Ledger} />

## Purpose
Every change to stock, and a way to record one: **what moved, when, and who did it**. Two tabs: the **ledger** and **Transfers** between places.

## What is here
- Ledger: `Ledger`, filters (`FilterStockOutSold`, `FilterWeek`, `FilterCustomDates`, `FilterSearch`), `JobOrderNote`.
- States: `Loading`, `ErrorState`, `NoMovements`, `NoResults`, `Quiet`.
- Record a movement: `RecordMovement`, `RecordMovementWithProduct`, `LandedWithUndo`, `UndoAsksFirst`, `RecordingOff`.
- Transfers list and filters: `Transfers`, `TransfersDraft`, `TransfersInTransit`, `TransfersReceived`, `TransfersCancelled`.
- One transfer by state: `TransferDraft`, `TransferInTransit`, `TransferSentOut`, `TransferReceived`, `TransferSendQuestion`, `TransferWaitingForManager`, `TransferCancelAsks`.
- Create or change: `NewTransfer`, `ChangeDraft`, `NewTransferWarning`.
- Receive a transfer: `ReceiveTransfer`, `ReceiveTransferShort`, `ReceiveTransferNothing`, `ReceiveTransferMixedUnits`, `ReceiveTransferError`.
- Who is looking: `Clerk`, `RecordMovementClerk`. Other tenant: `TenantB`.
- Phone 390: `Phone`, `PhoneRecord`, `PhoneTransfers`, `PhoneTransferPaper`, `PhoneReceiveTransfer`.

## Built from
Movement ledger, Record movement form, Transfer list / detail / form, and Step line, under Components / Inventory.
