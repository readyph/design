# Stocker

## Purpose
The phone app for the person on the shelves (handover 2.7). This is slice 1/6 (a): **the shared frame, tabs, steps and states**. Later slices add Find, Stock in, Stock out and Account.

## Layout
Header with the Portal R mark and platform name, account initials, a back chevron, the step line, the page title and lead; a scrolling main area; a pinned footer. Opens at phone width (390; `FrameSmall` is 360).

## What is here
`Frame`, `FrameSmall`, `Loading` (`?state=loading`), `ErrorState` (`?state=error`), `Empty` (`?state=empty`), `Quiet` (`?state=quiet`), `TenantB`.
