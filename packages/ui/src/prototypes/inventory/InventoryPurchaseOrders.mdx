# Purchase orders

<Canvas of={Stories.List} />

## Purpose
What have we ordered, what is late, and what actually arrived. Three views: the **list**, **one order**, and **Receive** (what actually arrived), plus the short-delivery block when a delivery comes in short.

## What is here
- List: `List`, `GroupBySupplier` (`&group=sup`), `NoPurchaseOrders`, `NoResults`, `Late`, `Quiet`, `EmptyStore`.
- One order, by state: `OrderDraft`, `OrderSent`, `OrderPartIn`, `OrderReceived`, `OrderCancelled`, `OrderCameInShort`, `OrderUndoReceipt`, `OrderLate`. Confirmations: `OrderDraftSendQuestion`, `OrderSentCancelQuestion`.
- Receive: `Receive`, `ReceivePartIn`, `PickDeliveryStory`.
- Short delivery: `ShortPartIn`, `ShortClosed`, `ShortAcceptForm`, `ShortDisputeForm`, `ShortAccepted`, `ShortDisputed`, `ShortRecordAnswer`, `ShortResolvedCredit`, `ShortError`.
- Who is looking: `Clerk`, `OrderClerk`, `OrderClerkDraft`, `ReceiveClerk`, `ShortClerk` (tell the manager), `ShortClerkTold`. Other tenant: `TenantB`, `OrderTenantB`, `ReceiveTenantB`.
- States: `Loading`, `ErrorState`.
- Print: `PrintOrder` (one order on one page), `PrintReceive` (Receive as a tally sheet).
- Phone 390: `Phone`, `OrderPhone`, `ReceivePhone`, `ShortPhone`.
