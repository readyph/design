# Customers (v2 Shop floor)

<Canvas of={Stories.Live} />

Live page: search a name, phone or plate (Enter opens a single match), click a row for the Customer page, Add car / Edit customer / Text / open a service, New service (wizard). **Open the story in the Canvas tab** for the Controls panel; the toolbar switches Viewport (phone) and Theme.

**States (Controls).** `scenario` (searches and views, batches, Customer pages for 1 / 2 / 3 cars, filtered, overdue, long name, car and customer sheets, wizard entry points), state (loading, first-run, error), inventory, panelCollapsed, leda, stress.

## Purpose
"Who is this, what have we done for them, and what do I do next?" Lookup first: the search box takes a name, phone or plate. The Customer page (full page, Back to the list) shows cars with their spec strips, open services, history and notes.

## Rationale
Source: `workshop/prototypes/sms/round-1/v2-floor/rationale.md` (Customers page, revisions 1-4). One ruled row per car; contact details are plain labelled lines; the Text capability was dropped from the Customer page; Add / Edit / Remove car keep the plate unique (duplicate plate names its owner).

## Assumptions
Page size 10; "next due" and "last service" are derived in sample data; role is not modelled here.

## Known weaknesses
- A long last-service title wraps to 2-3 lines in its spec column.
- Opening a service from a customer replaces the Customer page with the Inspector.
- Not browser-checked beyond tsc and the render test.

## API today vs invented
