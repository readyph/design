# Team

<Canvas of={Stories.Live} />

## Purpose
Who has access to what: **everyone, and a role on each platform**.

## Layout
- Place column: the map, where they work, roles by kind, Portal access, seats.
- Sheet: everyone as a table with a role per platform, grouped Waiting to join / Suspended / People.
- Person subpage: roles on each platform, Portal access, account, password, activity, suspend.
- Invite dialog: create, then send now or later.
- Leda: ask bar, palette, conversation, and her drafted invite.

## Guards
Own access, the creator, the last admin, suspend, and reset-link rules show their reason instead of acting.

## What is here
- Page and person: `Live`, `Person`, `PersonAdmin`, `PersonCreator`, `InviteDialog`.
- Who is looking: `Solo`, `NoAccess`, `Manager`.
- Scale: `OverSeats`, `ThreePlatforms`, `ManyPlatforms`.
- States: `Loading`, `ErrorState`. Phone: `Phone`, `PhonePerson`.
