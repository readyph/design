# Home

<Canvas of={Stories.Live} />

## Purpose
The Portal's front door: **what needs me today, across every platform?** The map and Needs you sit in the place column; the sheet shows Today, a single case, or the overview (greeting, Leda bar and chips, last 7 days, next, yesterday).

## What is here
- Opens on Today when something needs a decision (`Live`, four decisions). Click an item's button (or press Enter) to open its case, then Confirm.
- `Quiet` opens on the overview when nothing needs you.
- States: `Loading` (skeletons, Leda's mark plays Aurora), `PlatformError` (one platform fails, its block says so with Retry, the rest renders).
- Scale: `FewPlatforms`, `ManyPlatforms` and their quiet variants.
- Filters: `HqFilter`, `PlatformFilter`. Leda: `ConversationOpen`. `Phone` is the phone composition.

## Leda entry points
Ask bar, centred bar and chips, phone bar, palette, conversation panel, headline, proposals and watching, case note and proposal, Today blocks, status bar usage (L1 to L10).

## Shared chrome
The rail, the phone top bar (account and Leda) and the phone nav (labelled tabs and More) come from `PortalFrame` (`./PortalFrame.tsx`, not exported from the package). Memory, Platforms, Platform detail and Team use the same frame.
