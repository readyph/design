Light palette, ink chrome, semantic radius and density tokens. Adds `chrome` tokens (rail and sidebar are ink in both themes), a `[data-theme="light"]` scope, `radiusRole` (control 4 / panel 6 / modal 8) and `density` (control 32, sidebar item 28, table row 36 / dense 32, gutter 16 / 12). Card is flat with a hairline border by default; Badge `primary` is a blue tint (the `tint-peach` token is replaced by `tint-blue`); Button, Input and Select default to the 32px size; IconRail and Sidebar render on chrome.
